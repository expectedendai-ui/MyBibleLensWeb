Honest Answers

Honest Answers

The questions friends ask before they trust us with their faith practice.

Is this really one-time? No subscription tricks? +

Yes — really. You pay once on Apple's App Store or Google Play, and the tier you bought is yours for the lifetime of the app. We will never convert you to a subscription, never charge you a renewal, and never paywall a feature you already own. The founder built this app after a season of personal hardship — there will never be a "stress payment" inside MyBibleLens.

What happens if I lose my phone or get a new one? +

Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID or Google account — not the device. Sign in on any new device, tap "Restore Purchases" in Settings, and your full tier (and every seat you've shared) comes back instantly. Your reflections, mosaics, sermon decks, and study cards live in Supabase cloud sync — you won't lose a single page.

Will you add subscriptions or ads later? +

No. The business model is one-time IAP, full stop. New features that ship to the app are free forever for everyone who already owns a paid tier. We will never sell your data, run ads in the experience, or introduce a "Pro" subscription on top of your existing purchase. If economics change someday in a way that requires a new product, it will be a new app — not a subscription bolted onto this one.

Can I share it with my church or small group? +

Yes — that's exactly what the seat tiers are for. Sanctuary (3 seats) covers a household or prayer trio. Kingdom (7 seats) covers a small group. Founding (14 seats) covers a leadership team or extended fellowship. Everyone on a seat gets the full paid experience — same features, same sharing, same realtime collaboration. You assign seats by email or Display ID inside the app.

Is this for a specific denomination? +

MyBibleLens is built for anyone who reads the Bible. The AI is tuned to be reverent and scripturally grounded without leaning into any one denomination's distinctives. You'll find believers from Catholic, Protestant, Orthodox, and non-denominational backgrounds using it side by side. If a feature ever pushes a particular tradition, that's a bug — tell us at info@mybiblelens.us.

How is my data protected? +

Reflections, mosaics, and prayers are private by default — only you and the seats you've explicitly shared with can access them. Supabase Row-Level Security enforces this at the database level. AI requests are routed through our edge gateway with no third-party tracking, and we never sell or advertise against your spiritual content. Read the full Privacy Policy and AI Disclosure for details.

When does the app launch? +

MyBibleLens is currently in TestFlight private beta, with App Store + Google Play submission imminent. Follow @mybiblelens on Instagram for launch updates, or scroll up and tap the App Store / Google Play badges to be the first to know when it goes live.
