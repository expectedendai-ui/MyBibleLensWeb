What is S.O.A.P.?

A 4-step Bible study method — Lens guides you through each step with AI prompts. Here’s the flow on Philippians 4:13:

  • S Scripture. “I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.” — Philippians 4:13
  • O Observation. Paul wrote this from a prison cell. The strength isn’t self-generated — it flows through Christ.
  • A Application. When today feels too heavy, lean on His strength, not your own.
  • P Prayer. “Lord, when I’m at the end of myself, be my strength. Amen.”
