What this app will never have

What this app will never have:

  • ✕ No Gamification — your faith isn’t a game. Gamification limits you to the worth of that software… This app isn’t your limit. God is.
  • ✕ No AI Voice — God already speaks. You don’t need a robot to.
  • ✕ No Algorithm — nothing deciding what you see or when you see it.
  • ✕ No Explore Feed — no rabbit holes. No comparison traps.
  • ✕ No Scripture in-app — because it’s for your journal and your physical Bible. The Word belongs in your hands, not behind a screen.
  • ✕ No Companion — “Companion” is a sugarcoat.

    This app isn’t your friend. God is.

    Go to your church. Your pastor. Your friends and family — the people who actually love you.
  • ✕ No Selling Your Data — your prayers aren’t a product.

Just an efficient tool to grow your faith with God.
