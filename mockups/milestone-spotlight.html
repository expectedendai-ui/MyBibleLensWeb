Milestone turns “I should read the Bible more” into a plan with your name on it. Choose your goal — the whole Bible, Old Testament, or New Testament — pick the weekdays you actually read, and set the finish line. Milestone builds your day-by-day chapter path and tells you honestly what pace it takes. Then you just read, and check the days off — beside the people walking with you.

  • Your calendar, your pace — start date, finish date, and the weekdays you read
  • Day by day — exact chapters per reading day, with a minutes-a-day estimate
  • Honest pacing — gentle · steady · intense. Know before you commit
  • Quarter-mark milestones — see where 25%, 50%, and 75% land before you start
  • Reading Circle — family & fellowship check off each day beside you
  • Miss a day? Catch-up days are built in. No streaks. No XP. Just a finish line.
